7Product Scope and Prioritization

The full product consists of the 21 candidate capabilities below, proposed by the team. Every one of them is part of the product vision and none has been dropped. They are candidate requirements, not 21 implementation commitments: the MoSCoW priority and target release say what will be built for the MVP (31 Jan 2027) and what is scheduled later.

Complexity is a relative estimate (L = low, M = medium, H = high) of design, build and test effort for a student team new to Flutter. Release: MVP = by 31 Jan 2027. M5–M7 are post-MVP milestones (Section 15), not yet scheduled.

7.2 MVP: the first release we will implement in the course (by 31 Jan 2027)

The MVP is complete when every Must capability passes its acceptance criteria. Should capabilities are planned inside the MVP schedule and are the first to be cut if the schedule slips. Could capabilities are attempted only after all Must and Should items pass.

  • Must: sign-up and sign-in. Community membership approved by an administrator. Roles (F-07). Administrator gate management (simulated gates). One-time and recurring permissions (F-01, F-02) with a cumulative opening limit (F-03). Suspend, resume and cancel (F-04). A unique invitation link (F-08). A browser guest page with gate opening (F-09). The simulated gate and gate simulator. Resident activity log (F-13).
  • Should: multiple gates per permission (F-06). QR code (F-10). Push notifications (F-14). Home-screen dashboard (F-15). Rule-based guided creation wizard (F-21). Administrator community log. Link regeneration. Permission editing. Deep links to the app.
  • Could (stretch): templates (F-05). Contact directory (F-11). Natural-language creation (F-19). "My invitations" for registered guests. Expiry and last-opening reminders.

7.3 Developed later, after the MVP

  • M5 · Guest management: group invitations (F-12). Templates (F-05) and contact directory (F-11), if they were not delivered as stretch goals.
  • M6 · Community governance: administrator reports and analytics (F-16). Unusual-activity detection (F-17). Administrator approval for sensitive permissions (F-18).
  • M7 · AI: natural-language creation (F-19), if it was not delivered as a stretch goal. Smart reminders and suggestions (F-20). An AI-enhanced guided assistant (F-21).

7.4 Explicitly out of scope for this project

  • Integration with real gate hardware or controllers. All gates are simulated.
  • Residents' own entry and exit through Porta, and vehicle or license-plate recognition.
  • Video intercom, voice calls, and guard-station applications.
  • Payments, billing, and multi-community SaaS administration. One user belongs to one community in the MVP.
  • A separate desktop web admin portal. Administrator functions are in the mobile app in the MVP.
  • Opening a gate offline (deliberately prohibited, see NFR-14). Proximity or geofence verification (possible future work).
  • Legal compliance certification. Privacy principles are applied (Section 13), but no formal compliance audit is planned.

7.5 AI feasibility decision for the MVP

Proposed decision, pending team approval (Q-04): the MVP does not depend on any AI capability.

  1. F-21 Guided assistant enters the MVP as a deterministic, rule-based step-by-step wizard (Should). It delivers the guided experience with no external dependency, cost or unpredictability.
  2. F-19 Natural-language creation is a Could stretch goal. It is started only after every Must and Should item passes, and only if the team approves the LLM API cost (Budget Policy). Its design is safe by construction: the AI only drafts fields, the resident must confirm, and the system falls back to the manual form (FR-35, US-17).
  3. F-20 Smart reminders and suggestions is scheduled for M7. It needs usage history that the MVP will not have.

Why: about 10 weeks remain until 31 Jan 2027 and the team is new to Flutter. An external LLM adds paid usage, latency, non-deterministic output and a new failure mode (R-06). Putting the core access logic at risk for an optional feature would go against the project's first priority.

Course alignment: the team should confirm whether the course requires an AI feature inside the MVP (Q-04). If it does, F-19 moves from Could to Should, and F-15 and F-10 move down to Could to free up capacity.
